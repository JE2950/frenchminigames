# Les Mini-Jeux Français

Three Minecraft-style French escape games in one page. Open `index.html` in a browser, or turn on GitHub Pages for this repo. There is nothing to install.

Each game has three rooms with five locks in each, so 15 questions. A setup screen lets the teacher choose the topics and the question types: multiple choice, typed answers with accent buttons, or both.

| Mode | Setting | Grammar |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Le Louvre**: locked in overnight, past a sleeping guard and a talking Joconde | Adjective agreement and position: colours, regular, -e, irregular (heureux, gentil, génial…), beau/nouveau/vieux (bel, nouvel, vieil), and the BAGS rhyme |
| 2 | **L'école**: Madame Sévère has confiscated your pencil case | School subjects with le/la/l'/les, opinions and reasons (j'aime la musique parce que…), je suis fort(e) en…, and reading the timetable (à neuf heures et quart) |
| 3 | **La tour Eiffel**: the lift left without you, and the pigeons are on strike | Prepositions of place (with pictures), au/à la/à l'/aux, à/en/au/dans (en ville, dans ma ville, à Paris, en France), and directions on a map |

The vocabulary, genders and grammar come from the French department's own sheets:
- the CE vocabulary list and the IIs CE vocab pack;
- Adjectives (Scholarship French) and the written activity on adjectival agreement;
- Personal descriptions and the My school booklet;
- the whole-school matrix and the Dynamo 1 schemes of work (Modules 2 and 5).

Controls: WASD to walk, the mouse to look, E or a click to use, Esc to pause. On a tablet, use the on-screen stick and the USE button.
